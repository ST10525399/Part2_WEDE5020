About Us

Our story

History

Founded in 2014 as a small workshop in Gauteng, Heritage & Hand Carpentry began with a simple goal: to restore the dedication to handcrafted quality in modern furniture. Over the past decade, we have expanded from miniature repairs to delivering bespoke interior joinery, custom cabinetry, and full structural fitting solutions across South Africa.

Mission

Our mission is to transform living and commercial spaces through exceptional woodworking. We are dedicated to using reliable materials, precise execution, and personal client collaboration to build functional, beautiful woodwork tailored to each client's vision.

Vision

We aim to be South Africa's premier bespoke carpentry brand—setting the benchmark for sustainable craftsmanship, design excellence, and lifelong durability in every piece we create.
