Services

What we offer

Custom Furniture

We design and handcraft bespoke wooden furniture tailored precisely to your space and personal style, including dining tables, bed frames, and study desks. Utilizing sustainably sourced hardwoods like oak, walnut, and teak, our craftsmen combine traditional joinery with modern finishes to create durable, timeless pieces. The complete process—from initial sketch approval and timber selection to final oiling—typically takes 3 to 5 weeks depending on project complexity.

Fitted Cabinetry & Joinery

Maximize your interior space with fully custom built-in wardrobes, kitchen units, media walls, and alcove shelving. We work directly with homeowners and interior designers to build seamless storage solutions using high-end moisture-resistant MDF, solid wood veneers, and soft-close hardware. Every project includes full 3D design and planning to ensure a perfect fit and finish, usually completed within 2 to 4 weeks.

Repairs & Restoration

Breathe new life into heirloom furniture, damaged wooden fixtures, and structural timber with our expert restoration services. We specialize in structural stabilization, veneer repair, color matching, and hand-rubbed French polishing, preserving original craftsmanship while restoring functionality. Whether repairing water-damaged kitchen cabinets or refinishing antique dining sets, our typical turnaround time ranges from 5 to 10 business days.

Decking & Outdoor Structures

Extend your living space outdoors with custom-built wooden decks, pergolas, garden gates, and exterior cladding. We utilize weather-resistant timbers such as Balau, Treated Pine, and high-performance composite decking boards paired with stainless-steel concealed fasteners for long-lasting durability against the elements.
